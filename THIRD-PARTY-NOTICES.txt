jsmn

Json-Rpc/Jsmn/JsmnTokenizer.cs is a C# port of the jsmn JSON tokenizer by Serge A.
Zaitsev (ported from https://github.com/zserge/jsmn at commit 25647e6, 2021-10-14),
adapted to ReadOnlySpan<byte> with grammar validation added. It is used by the built-in
serializer (AustinHarris.JsonRpc.Jsmn.JsmnSerializer) and ships in every build of the
AustinHarris.JsonRpc assembly.
